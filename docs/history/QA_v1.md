# Q&A sheet (judges' likely questions, honest answers)

**Why not just a deepfake detector?** Because detectors don't transfer. Pretrained AASIST misses 56% of synthetic clips
on held-out attacks of the very dataset it was trained on (ASVspoof 2019 LA eval), and our own trained add-on fell to
chance (AUC 0.46) on new data. A scam also works with a real human voice. The strongest evidence is not the voice: it is
*who receives the money*. VERIQ checks the payee name your own bank app shows against the firm the chat claims.

**What if the name matches?** Then the answer is at best "No major issue", which always says "This is not a guarantee".
A mule account can be opened in the firm's exact name (a known gap in our red-team set), and pressure cues such as
"pay 18% tax to withdraw" still make the answer "Inconclusive: don't pay yet".

**What does a genuine user go through?** If a real broker is flagged (scenario D: the bank shows a short trading name),
the user taps "ask for review"; a reviewer checks SEBI and overturns it; the original answer and an audit trail without
raw identifiers are kept, and the time to decision is shown. VERIQ itself blocks nothing and contacts no one.

**How would you evade it?** Open the mule account in the firm's exact name; avoid every pressure keyword; send the
payment link outside the chat; use a voice engine we have not tested. All are listed in the evasion lab with what we
would change.

**Who pays for it?** No paid API: local models and rules on a laptop or a small server. The real costs are reviewer time
at a bank fraud desk or cyber cell and keeping the SEBI snapshot fresh (estimates, see DEPLOYABILITY.md).

**What don't you know?** Accuracy on real victims' chats; voice detection on Hindi, real WhatsApp notes and real clones;
how users react to "Inconclusive"; whether banks would share the registered payee name directly.

**Is any number from real victims?** No. Chats are synthetic (generated by us) or written blind by teammates; voice
data is public (ASVspoof, FSDD) or synthetic (Kokoro). Every number in RESULTS.md says which.

**Did you build all this in 24 hours?** The declared prototype is tag `v0-prebuilt` (PREBUILT.md); everything after it is
finale work, commit by commit (FINALE_LOG.md). Claude (Anthropic) wrote code under our direction; DISCLOSURE.md lists
every model, dataset and library with its licence.
